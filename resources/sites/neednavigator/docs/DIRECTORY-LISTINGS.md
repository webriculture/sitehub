Paste as-is. Roughly 1,400 characters, which fits every form I know of.

> Need Navigator is case management software for human services organizations: community
> action agencies, shelters, food banks, parent education providers and other community aid
> organizations.
>
> Every agency runs on its own copy of the application with its own private database. Nothing
> is pooled with other agencies, and the system is configured to your programs, forms, roles

>
> Staff manage client records, households, care teams, income and eligibility, assistance
> requests, referrals, visits, goals, documents, shelter beds and classes in one place. The

>
> Highlights include an emergency assistance workflow that ties every dollar to a funding pool
> and prints the eligibility math on the voucher; income screening against federal poverty
> level and area median income thresholds, keeping staff-verified income separate from
> self-reported; a live submissions grid the whole team works in at once, with a matching
> engine that turns applications into client records or whole households without retyping;
> shelter floor plans and reservations; parent education classes from catalog to certificate;
> and a report builder with PII redaction and scheduled delivery.
>
> Pricing is published: $25 per user per month, five-user minimum, plus a one-time setup fee
> from $2,000 covering implementation and up to 12 hours of virtual training.

## 9. Reviews: the part that actually moves you

Position in the unpaid sort is driven mostly by review count and rating, and G2 will not place
you on a Grid report until you clear its review threshold. This is where you have an advantage
most small vendors do not: agencies that like you.

- Ask 8 to 10 people, one at a time, by name. A generic blast gets ignored.
- Ask the person who uses it daily, not the executive director who signed.
- Send the direct link to the review form, not the homepage.
- Do **not** offer anything in exchange. Both platforms police incentivised reviews, and a
  purge would cost you more than the reviews are worth.
- Reviewers are verified by work email or LinkedIn, so warn them it takes a few minutes.

## 10. Do not claim (binding, same as the website)

- Online payment for event registrations or donations. Stripe is chosen, not finished.
- Event waitlists. Plumbing only.
- Document Manager and partner document sharing. Beta.
- The Need Navigator Network as an active network. It is a capability; no agency pairs are
  federating.
- Electronic claim or remittance submission. The exported batch is the hand-off.
- Any named-framework compliance claim: HIPAA, HMIS certification. Say "HMIS-adjacent data
  model" at most.
- Bulk client-data import as a product feature. Migrations are a quoted service.
- Client names. Never, on any platform, in any field.
