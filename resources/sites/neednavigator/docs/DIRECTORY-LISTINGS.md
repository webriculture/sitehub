## 9. Reviews: the part that actually moves you

Position in the unpaid sort is driven mostly by review count and rating, and G2 will not place
you on a Grid report until you clear its review threshold. This is where you have an advantage
most small vendors do not: agencies that like you.

- Ask 8 to 10 people, one at a time, by name. A generic blast gets ignored.
- Ask the person who uses it daily, not the executive director who signed.
- Send the direct link to the review form, not the homepage.
- Do **not** offer anything in exchange. Both platforms police incentivised reviews, and a
  purge would cost you more than the reviews are worth.
- Reviewers are verified by work email or LinkedIn, so warn them it takes a few minutes.

### HIPAA — reversed 2026-09-05, tick the box
Dave established that **Webriculture signs BAAs** and has operated as a Business Associate for
a health system. There is no HIPAA certification, so the checkbox's working meaning is "usable
with PHI, BAA available" — which is true. The posture supports it: role/program/team-scoped
access control, unique user IDs, SAML + passkeys + 2FA + IP allowlisting, HTTPS enforced,
AES-256 at rest (encryption is *addressable* under the Security Rule, not required), and an
audit trail that logs every record **view** with user, time, IP and device plus eight
suspicious-access rules.

Still off-limits: the word **certified**, and any unscoped encryption claim. BACKUP-CLAIMS.md's
prohibition was written about backup wording specifically; it does not reach this checkbox.

**Two follow-ups this exposed:**
1. Confirm whether the two secondary servers with unencrypted volumes (FEATURES.md Decisions

2. **BAAs appear nowhere on the website.** The security page is careful about encryption and
